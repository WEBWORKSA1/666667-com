Quick answer: Look for numbers ending in 8, 6 or 9, with repeating lucky digits (88, 666, 8888) and no 4. Avoid 14, 74 and 748. Patterns like AABB and ABAB are prized.

The record-breakers

Lucky numbers can be expensive. MediaOptions reports that a Hong Kong licence plate consisting only of the digit 8 sold for around US$6 million, and that a Chengdu phone number made up of 8s sold for around US$270,000. Most people won't pay that — but the same principles help you pick a better number for free.

What to look for

  • The ending counts most. People remember and repeat the last four digits. Endings like 8888, 6688, 1688, 5188 and 9999 are top-tier.
  • Repeats. AAA and AAAA runs of 6, 8 or 9 are valued.
  • Patterns. AABB (6688), ABAB (6868) and ascending sequences (5678, “step by step higher”) are easy to remember.
  • No 4. Especially not 14 (“will die”) or 74 (气死, “furious to death”).

Licence plates
