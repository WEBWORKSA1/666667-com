Quick answer: Chinese netizens type numbers that sound like phrases: 520 = I love you, 666 = awesome, 233 = LOL, 88 / 886 = bye, 1314 = forever, 555 = crying, 748 = go die.

Love codes

  • 520 — 我爱你, I love you (May 20 is an unofficial Valentine's Day)
  • 521 — 我愿意, I do
  • 530 — 我想你, I miss you
  • 1314 — 一生一世, for a lifetime; 5201314 — I love you forever
  • 3344 — 生生世世, life after life
