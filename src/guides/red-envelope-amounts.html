Quick answer: Use even amounts built from 6, 8 and 9 (e.g. 88, 168, 288, 666, 888, 999). Never give amounts containing 4, and avoid 250. For weddings, 9s (longevity) and 520/1314 (love) are popular.

General rules

  • Even numbers are preferred for happy occasions (good things come in pairs). Odd amounts are traditionally associated with funerals.
  • No 4 anywhere: 40, 400, 444 are all poor choices.
  • New, crisp notes are a sign of respect.
  • Amounts vary hugely by region, city and closeness of the relationship — local custom always wins.
