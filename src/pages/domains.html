Numbers are the universal language of the Chinese internet.

Numeric domains need no translation, no pinyin and no English. They work across dialects, type fast on any keyboard, and carry lucky meanings — which is why Chinese companies and investors have paid premiums for them for over a decade.

What makes a numeric domain premium

  • Length: fewer digits = rarer (only 100 two-digit and 1,000 three-digit .coms exist)
  • No 4: 4 sounds like “death”; many buyers avoid it entirely
  • “Chinese premium” digits: names without 0 or 4 are a sought-after class
  • Patterns: AAAA, AABB, ABAB and long repeating runs
  • Meaning: 168, 518, 520, 888, 1314, 666 read as phrases
  • Extension: .com leads; .cn and .net follow at a discount
