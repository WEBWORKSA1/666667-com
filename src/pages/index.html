Why “666667”?

Smooth, smooth, smooth, smooth, smooth — then rise.

In Chinese, 6 (六, liù) sounds like 溜 / 流 — smooth, flowing, skilful. Online, 666 is how hundreds of millions of people say “awesome, well played”. 7 (七, qī) echoes 起 (qǐ, “rise”). So 666667 reads like a wish: everything flows… and then takes off.

Bonus trivia: 666667 is a prime number, and 0.666667 is 2/3 rounded to six decimals.

FAQ

Common questions

Why are numbers lucky or unlucky in Chinese culture?

Mandarin and Cantonese have many homophones. A number “inherits” the meaning of a word it sounds like: 8 (bā) ≈ 发 (fā, prosper), 4 (sì) ≈ 死 (sǐ, death), 9 (jiǔ) ≈ 久 (jiǔ, long-lasting), 6 (liù) ≈ 溜 (liū, smooth).

What is the luckiest number in China?

8 is widely considered the luckiest, followed by 6 and 9. Combinations like 168, 518, 888 and 1688 are especially popular for businesses.

What does 666 mean in Chinese?

Online, 666 (liù liù liù) means “awesome, well played, so smooth”. It is praise — the opposite of its Western association.

Is the lucky score scientific?

No. It is a cultural heuristic built from widely shared homophone meanings. It is useful for marketing and naming decisions, not for predicting outcomes.
