🔢 Lucky Number Checker

How the score works

  1. Each digit gets a cultural weight (8 = +4, 6 and 9 = +3, 2 = +2, 4 = −4…).
  2. Known combinations (168, 518, 520, 1314, 748, 250…) add or subtract points.
  3. Repeated lucky digits (888, 6666) earn a bonus; any 4 costs points.
  4. The last digit counts extra for prices, plates and phone numbers.

Scores are a transparent heuristic for naming and marketing decisions — not fortune-telling or financial advice.
