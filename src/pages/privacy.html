Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

What we collect

  • Information you submit in forms (name, email, optional phone/WeChat, company, message, and form-specific details such as numbers you want reviewed or donation pledges).
  • Tool inputs (numbers, years, prices) are processed in your browser and are not sent to us unless you include them in a form.
  • Usage data via analytics (if enabled): pages viewed, device type, approximate location, referrer.
  • Cookies from advertising partners such as Google AdSense (if enabled), and small local-storage preferences (theme, cookie choice).

How we use it

To answer your request, deliver audits and newsletters you ask for, run contests, process pledges, improve the site, and show ads that keep it free. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to our private inbox by a form-processing service (FormSubmit). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Analytics and ads, when enabled, are provided by Google. Payments, when made, are handled entirely by the payment provider you choose (e.g. PayPal, Stripe) — we never receive card numbers.

Your choices & rights

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time, choose “Essential only” in the cookie notice, opt out of personalised ads at Google Ads Settings, and request access, correction or deletion of your data through our contact form. Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25 in Canada, CCPA in California), you may have additional rights.

Retention

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping (normally up to 24 months), then delete them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and contests are limited to adults.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
