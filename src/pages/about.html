About us

We decode the numbers 1.4 billion people read differently.

666667 is an independent website about Chinese number culture — the homophones, traditions and internet slang that make 8 lucky, 4 unlucky and 666 a compliment.

Our mission

Make number culture understandable, useful and fun: for travellers and learners, for couples picking a wedding date, and for businesses that want to price, name and launch products the smart way for Chinese-speaking customers anywhere in the world.

What we do

  • Free tools — lucky number checker, price generator, zodiac finder, numeric domain valuator.
  • A living dictionary of number meanings and slang, with pronunciations and FAQs.
  • Guides and videos that explain the “why” behind each number.
  • Free business audits and paid follow-up packages.
  • Community — monthly contests, supporter memberships and open roles for talent.

Editorial standards

We cross-check meanings against multiple reputable sources, note regional differences (Mandarin vs Cantonese, mainland vs diaspora), and label anything that is folk belief or internet slang. Lucky scores are transparent heuristics, not predictions. Sponsored content is always labelled.
