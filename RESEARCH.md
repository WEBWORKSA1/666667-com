# 666667.com — Research, Idea Selection & Monetization Strategy

_Prepared September 2026._

## 1. What "666667" means in Chinese culture, the economy and to people

| Layer | Finding | Implication for the site |
|---|---|---|
| **Digit 6 (六 liù)** | Homophone of 溜 (liū, "smooth / slick / skilled") and 流 (liú, "flow"). Idiom 六六大顺 = "everything goes smoothly". Favoured for business, openings, weddings. | Strong positive brand story. |
| **"666" slang** | The most common Chinese internet praise: "awesome / well played". Started in gaming, spread to livestreams, short video comments (danmu) and everyday chat. | Huge, evergreen search and social demand ("666 meaning"). |
| **Digit 7 (七 qī)** | Echoes 起 (qǐ, "rise / start") and 齐 (qí, "together"). Qixi (七夕) is Chinese Valentine's Day. Caveat: the 7th lunar month is Ghost Month. | 666667 = "smooth ×5, then rise". |
| **No 4** | 4 (sì) sounds like 死 (sǐ, death). Tetraphobia shapes floors, prices, phone numbers and plates. | 666667 is "clean". The anti-4 angle drives the B2B audit offer. |
| **Economy: numeric assets** | Lucky plates, phone numbers and numeric domains trade at premiums. MediaOptions reports 55.com at ~$2.3M, 114.com at ~$2.1M, 88888.com at ~$245K. It also reports a Hong Kong "8" plate at ~$6M and a Chengdu all-8s phone number at ~$270K. | Supports a numeric-domain desk and valuation tool that bring in high-value leads. |
| **Why numbers online** | Chinese input requires pinyin or stroke IMEs. Numbers need no translation and work across dialects. Examples: 163.com and 360.cn. | Makes the domain itself credible as a China-savvy brand. |
| **Maths trivia** | 666667 is **prime**. 0.666667 is 2/3 rounded to 6 decimals. | Shareable, linkable facts. |
| **Western risk** | 666 = "number of the beast", and "667 = neighbour of the beast" is a known joke. | The hero and meta descriptions lead with the Chinese meaning. |

**Trademark check:** web searches found no brand, app or company using "666667". This does not replace a formal search of USPTO, CNIPA and the WIPO Global Brand Database (classes 9, 35, 36, 41 and 45) before scaling. The site uses the number only descriptively and carries a full disclosure (`legal.html`).

**Winner: A.** It captures evergreen informational search ("what does 520 mean", "is 4 unlucky", "lucky phone number checker", "chinese zodiac lucky numbers"). It turns that traffic into AdSense and YouTube revenue, and routes the commercial slice into two lead streams:

1. B2B audits of prices, phone numbers, addresses, names and launch dates. The buyers are retailers, real-estate firms, restaurants, auto dealers and brands serving Chinese-speaking customers.
2. The numeric-domain desk, for buyers, sellers and appraisals.

It avoids gambling and lottery content, which AdSense restricts.

## 4. Benchmark: 29 sites visited

**Chinese number content:** chinahighlights, travelchinaguide, chinatravel, mandarinzone, thatsmandarin, yourchineseastrology, fengshuinexus, omniglot, mdbg.

**Tool funnels:** numerology.com, cafeastrology, horoscope.com, astrology.com, whats-your-sign.

**Calculators:** omnicalculator, calculator.net, timeanddate.

**Slang:** urbandictionary, knowyourmeme.

**China media:** whatsonweibo, sixthtone.

**Domains:** dnjournal, domainnamewire, afternic (dan.com redirects here), escrow.com, mediaoptions.

**Support and contests:** buymeacoffee, ko-fi, gleam.io.

GoFundMe and Kickstarter were reviewed for crowdfunding patterns.

**Patterns adopted:**

- A tool in the hero with example chips (calculator.net, Omni).
- A quick-answer box at the top of each guide, a colour-coded lucky/unlucky verdict, and an FAQ (mandarinzone).
- Free result first, with the lead CTA after the result (numerology.com).
- A split-audience domain CTA ("I own it / I want to buy") and trust signals built from real sales figures (mediaoptions, escrow).
- A sponsor rate card and an "advertise here" page (dnjournal, domainnamewire).
- Preset donation amounts, a supporter wall and monthly tiers (Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi).
- A countdown, entry form, bonus visibility and published rules (gleam).
- One page per term, laid out like a dictionary entry: characters, pinyin, origin, usage, related terms (Urban Dictionary, KYM, thatsmandarin).
- Schema markup: FAQPage, DefinedTerm, Article, BreadcrumbList, WebApplication.
